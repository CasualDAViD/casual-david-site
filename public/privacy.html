Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

This policy explains how Casual_DAViD ("we") handles personal data on casualdavid.com.ng. It is written for the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA). The contact for privacy requests is munachimsoogun@gmail.com.

Who is responsible

Casual_DAViD decides why and how your data is used for this site and shop. That makes us the data controller for the NDPA. We do not have a separate postal address published here. Email is the way to reach us.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, and a sign-in provided by Firebase Authentication (email and password, or Google). We do not see or store your password. Firebase stores a hash of it.
  • Purchase details: the mod you bought, the amount, a payment reference, and the time. Card and bank details are entered on Paystack's page. We do not receive or store your card number.
  • Files you download after a purchase, and support emails you send us.
  • Technical data needed to run the site: IP address for rate limits on the shop server, and a theme or currency choice saved in your browser.

Why we use it

  • To create your account and keep you signed in.
  • To take payment, deliver the file, and keep a record of the sale.
  • To reply to support and refund requests.
  • To protect the site from abuse (rate limits, admin access checks).

Under the NDPA, the usual basis is that the processing is needed to perform the contract (your account and purchase), or that it is our legitimate interest in keeping the shop secure. We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

Who else receives it

  • Google Firebase stores accounts and the shop catalogue data.
  • Paystack processes the payment.
  • Cloudflare stores uploaded images and paid files for the shop.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the public pages.
  • If you email us, your message is handled by our email provider.

Some of these providers store data outside Nigeria. Where the NDPA requires it, we rely on the provider's safeguards for cross-border transfers and only send what the feature needs.

How long we keep it

Account and purchase records are kept while your account is open and for as long as we need them for tax, refunds, and disputes. You can ask us to delete your account. We may keep a purchase record where the law requires a sales record, with the rest of the profile removed. Server logs used for rate limits are short-lived.

Your rights

Under the NDPA you can ask us to:

  • confirm whether we hold your data and give you a copy
  • correct data that is wrong
  • delete data we no longer need
  • limit or object to a use that is not required for the purchase
  • take a complaint to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng)

Email munachimsoogun@gmail.com with the address on your account. We may need to confirm it is you before we change or delete anything.

Children

The shop is not aimed at children. Do not create an account if you are under 18 unless a parent or guardian agrees. If we learn we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it.

Cookies

See the cookie notice. The theme and currency choices live in local storage on your device. Sign-in on the Node shop, when that server is in use, uses an HttpOnly cookie.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change and the new version will be on this page.
