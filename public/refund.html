Refund Policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

This policy covers paid mods, plugins, and addons bought on casualdavid.com.ng. Payments are taken by Paystack in Nigerian naira. Email munachimsoogun@gmail.com with your payment reference if you need a refund.

Digital goods

The product is a digital file. Delivery is the download link after Paystack confirms the payment. Because the file can be copied as soon as it is downloaded, a change-of-mind refund is not available once the file has been delivered and it works as described.

When we refund

  • You were charged twice for the same order.
  • Payment succeeded but the file was never made available, and we cannot fix that.
  • The file is materially different from the description on its product page (not a version or modpack mismatch), and we cannot provide a working copy.
  • A refund is required by Nigerian consumer law.

When we do not refund

  • You changed your mind after downloading a file that matches its description.
  • The file does not work with a modpack, server, or Minecraft version that the product page did not promise to support.
  • A server bans you, or your world has trouble, because of how the mod was used. Back up before you install. See the Terms of Use.
  • You shared the file and then asked for a refund.

How to ask

Email munachimsoogun@gmail.com within 14 days of the payment. Include the Paystack reference, the mod name, and what went wrong. We will reply by email. Approved refunds are sent back through Paystack to the original payment method. Paystack's own timing applies after we approve it.

Statutory rights under the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria are not removed by this policy.
