Cookie notice

Last updated 23 November 2026

Casual_DAViD uses a small amount of browser storage so the site can remember your settings and, when you sign in, who you are. We do not use advertising cookies. Questions: munachimsoogun@gmail.com. The Privacy Policy explains the rest of the personal data we hold.

On this device

  • Theme (cd_theme) remembers light, dark, or system. Stored in local storage.
  • Currency (cd_currency) remembers USD, NGN, or EUR for price display. Stored in local storage.
  • This notice (cd_cookie_notice) remembers that you dismissed the banner.
  • Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication, which keeps its own session in the browser's storage. That is how you stay signed in on the public site.

Cookies on the shop server

If the Node shop server is what is serving your visit, signing in also sets a cookie named cd_session. It is HttpOnly (JavaScript on the page cannot read it), SameSite=Lax, and Secure when the site is on HTTPS. A short-lived cd_oauth cookie is used only while a Google or Discord sign-in is in progress, with the same flags. These cookies are how the server knows the account. They are not used for ads.

How to turn them off

You can clear site data for casualdavid.com.ng in your browser settings. That signs you out and resets the theme. Blocking all cookies will also sign you out of the shop server. The public pages still load without an account.
